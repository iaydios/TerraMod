# Skill Text: Story-Style Draft v4 (not yet applied, pending Sam's confirmation)

v4 is rewritten according to Sam's corrections and is simpler: background is one line only; effect text is unchanged.
- Spear Grave: the long spears stab in so thickly that it looks like a graveyard.
- Out of the Way: a heavy motorcycle from the Gugba Ride [格古巴騎].
- Quake Slash: has nothing to do with ice fields or frozen caves.
- Gugba Earthquake: deliberately stomping with the full force of the body, not energy from a fall.
- Kana: all use the original skill descriptions (keeping the original line that puzzles readers, with no extra effect description added).
- Y'apkar: focuses on the highly intelligent machine, and on his careful study that makes his delusions come true.
