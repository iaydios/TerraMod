Story basis:
- Ma'curi・Λ: a body remade into a half-machine; nanomachines in his blood numb pain and stitch wounds; the mechanical arm welded onto him is made from dreadnought armor; the ancient, broken long spear at his waist went through the same hell as he did; he is searching for an opponent strong enough to make him bleed.
- Kana・Λ: a quiet scholar transformed into a queen bee; taverns and dance halls are her new domain; the underworld calls her the Queen Bee, and none of those she charms escape.

- Y'apkar?・Λ: a delusion of "Knight Saves the Damsel," years of studying magic and machinery under Doctor Soman [索曼醫生], repairing high-HP machines, from which the princess emerges.
Format: one line of background + the original effect text (effects unchanged).
